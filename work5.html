Itinerary planner/tracker mobile App

Overview

TripMaster is a centralized travel management application designed to organize itineraries and finances into a clean, card-based dashboard. It allows users to track current activities, such as hotel check-ins, while monitoring a dynamic "Total Trip Budget" via a live progress bar. The app also organizes sequential travel events, like return flights and local excursions, with quick-action buttons for real-time updates.

The Goal:The primary goal is to reduce travel-related stress by combining schedule tracking and financial management in one place. By providing a clear roadmap of both time-sensitive plans and remaining funds, the app helps travelers stay organized and within their budget throughout their journey.

Process

I began by architecting a scalable data model for itinerary items, followed by building the core dashboard logic. A critical phase involved developing the AddItem component, where I integrated input validation and a callback system (onActionComplete) to ensure seamless data synchronization between the entry form and the global trip state.

Key Takeaway

I learned the importance of state synchronization in mobile development. By building a functional budget tracker, I discovered that user trust is built through immediate UI feedback—where every added expense or activity instantly updates the application’s state without lag or data loss.

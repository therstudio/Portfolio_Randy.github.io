Vaulty

Itinerary planner/tracker mobile App

Overview

Vaulty is a high-fidelity fintech solution designed to solve the growing problem of "subscription fatigue." In an era where digital services are fragmented, Vaulty provides a centralized, secure environment for users to track, analyze, and manage their recurring expenses.

The Goal:The goal of Vaulty is to empower users to reclaim control over their digital finances by centralizing subscription management into a single, high-security dashboard. By leveraging automated tracking and intuitive visual analytics, the app aims to eliminate "subscription fatigue" and prevent financial leaks caused by forgotten free trials or redundant services. Through a premium dark-mode interface and dynamic budgeting logic, Vaulty transforms complex transaction data into actionable insights, ultimately helping users save money and streamline their monthly recurring expenses with confidence.

Role & Tech

  • UX/UI Designer & Researcher
  • Figma, Behance (Moodboarding)

Process

My workflow followed a structured UX/UI lifecycle, moving from fictional user research and persona development to the creation of a sitemap and user flows. I translated these requirements into 11 high-fidelity screens using a "Deep Space" visual theme. The process centered on brand authenticity, featuring custom hand-drawn iconography and a comprehensive UI Kit. To ensure a technically rigorous prototype, I implemented advanced Figma variables and expressions, enabling dynamic budget calculations and real-time state changes that simulate a live application environment.

Key Takeaway

I learned how to bridge the gap between static interface design and functional logic by leveraging computational design; I discovered that a financial app’s true value lies in how real-time data feedback and physical interactions (like overlays and variables) empower a user’s sense of agency and trust over their own capital.

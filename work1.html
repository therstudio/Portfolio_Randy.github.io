UI/UX Web Design: E-Commerce sustainable Clothing Site

Digital Design & Development Case Study

Overview

This is a high-fidelity and low-fidelity wireframe project of a sustainable E-commerce website made on Figma.

The Goal: I designed a sustainable e-commerce platform by first using low-fidelity wireframes to prioritize a transparent, ethical user journey, ensuring that impact data was as accessible as product pricing.

View Live Demo

Project Breakdown

Role & Tech

  • UX/UI Designer & Researcher
  • Figma, Behance (Moodboarding)

Process

My process began with a Behance moodboard to establish the "Cosmic-Eco" aesthetic, followed by low-fidelity wireframes in Figma to map out a transparent shopping journey.

Key Takeaway

I learned how to use character-driven storytelling and a bold, cosmic aesthetic to make sustainability feel engaging rather than intimidating.

Sustainable Clothing E-Commerce

UI/UX Web Design & Case Study

Overview

A high-fidelity and low-fidelity wireframe project of a sustainable E-commerce website designed in Figma. This project explores the intersection of ethical shopping and modern "Cosmic-Eco" aesthetics.

The Goal: To prioritize a transparent, ethical user journey, ensuring that environmental impact data is as accessible and easy to understand as product pricing.

View Figma Prototype

Project Breakdown

Role & Tech

  • UX/UI Designer
  • User Researcher
  • Figma & Behance

Process

I began with moodboarding to establish the aesthetic, followed by low-fidelity wireframes to map the journey. The final stage involved building high-fidelity screens with a focus on data transparency.

Key Takeaway

I learned how to use bold aesthetics to make sustainability engaging. Making "green" data visual and character-driven significantly improves user agency and trust.

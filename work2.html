Immersive Tech: VR Murder Mystery Escape Room

Digital Design & Development Case Study

Overview

An immersive VR experience that blends high-stakes deduction with escape room mechanics, challenging players to uncover a killer while solving tactile, physics-based puzzles. Using high-fidelity environmental storytelling, the game leverages spatial audio and 360-degree interaction to plunge players into a high-tension mystery where every object is a potential clue.

The Goal: My objective was to design a high-fidelity VR environment that transforms the player from a passive observer into an active investigator. By merging escape room logic with a deep murder mystery narrative, I intended to leverage spatial presence to create a visceral sense of urgency and environmental storytelling where every physical interaction reveals a piece of the hidden truth.

Role & Tech

  • VR Experience Designer & Narrative Architect

Process

My workflow involved establishing an atmospheric moodboard for the crime scene, designing spatial UX flows in Figma for intuitive 360-degree interactions, and building a high-fidelity VR prototype that prioritizes environmental storytelling and physics-based clue discovery.

Key Takeaway

I learned how to leverage spatial presence and environmental storytelling to create an immersive mystery, discovering that the most effective VR puzzles are those where the player’s physical intuition directly uncovers the narrative.

VR Murder Mystery Escape Room

Immersive Tech & Narrative Design Case Study

Overview

An immersive VR experience that blends high-stakes deduction with escape room mechanics, challenging players to uncover a killer while solving tactile, physics-based puzzles.

The Goal: To transform the player from a passive observer into an active investigator using high-fidelity spatial presence and environmental storytelling.

Project Demo

Project Breakdown

Role & Tech

  • VR Experience Designer
  • Narrative Architect
  • Unity / C#

Process

Workflow involved atmospheric moodboarding, spatial UX flow design in Figma, and building a physics-based VR prototype for tactile clue discovery.

Key Takeaway

I learned that effective VR puzzles rely on physical intuition—when the player's natural movement reveals the story, the immersion is complete.

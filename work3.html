Responsive Medical Website Secondaid

Digital Design & Development Case Study

Overview

SecondAid is a full-stack e-commerce solution designed to make healthcare more accessible by providing a secure platform for buying and selling second-hand medical products. By integrating a robust backend with a user-friendly interface, the project ensures that life-saving equipment remains affordable and sustainable through a circular economy.

The Goal: My goal was to bridge the gap between medical affordability and web security, creating a high-trust marketplace where users can confidently trade certified second-hand medical equipment."

  • HTML & CSS

Process

My workflow involved architecting the backend logic and database schema to manage product listings, followed by developing the frontend to ensure a clean, clinical user interface. I then integrated the two using RESTful APIs and deployed the full-stack application onto Render, utilizing its hosting services to manage a live environment with persistent data.

Key Takeaway

I learned the intricacies of full-stack deployment and the importance of server-side validation, gaining hands-on experience in maintaining a live web service that requires 24/7 uptime and data integrity.
